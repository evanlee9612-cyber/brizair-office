## Parts selling-price rule (from the old Parts Inventory Excel, "PRICE RANGE" box)
LG cost (RATE LG MANILA) → customer price:
- ₱0 – 499: cost × 3 + 12% VAT
- ₱500 – 1,499: cost × 2.50 + 12% VAT
- ₱1,500 – 2,999: cost × 2 + 12% VAT
- ₱3,000 and above: cost × 1.75 + 12% VAT
- Rounding: last digit 0–4 → no round-off (end 0); last digit 5–9 → round off (add 1).
The PARTS tab "Customer Price w/ VAT" already has the approved price for most parts — use that first. Use the rule only when the price is blank, and say it is computed.

## The office website (BRIZAIR Office) — what each button does
Login: each person has a 6-digit PIN. Phone: add the site to the Home Screen. "?" (top right) = help for the screen you are on. "EN / 한" (top right) = language. Phone bottom bar: Home · Schedule · Ask GPT · Stock · Customers. Your name (top right) = More menu (Money, Quotes & Invoices, LG Monthly Report, Materials for AI, Activity, Language, Help). Round "+" button = New (any record). On a computer the same menu is on the left.
- Home → quick buttons (New job, Unit in / out, Part in / out, Payment, Cleaning done, Quotation) and a "To do" list (old jobs without status, overdue invoices, quotations to follow up, customers due for cleaning).
- New job → adds a job to SCHEDULE (date, time, job type, customer, location, units, technician). Open the job to change status: Scheduled → Confirmed → Done (or Moved / Cancelled), add job photos (saved to Google Drive), print Service Report or Start-up Report.
- Unit in / out → one row in UNIT IN-OUT (IN = delivery from supplier, OUT = installed / sold). Stock NOW updates by itself. A unit sold with a front panel = 2 entries (unit + panel).
- Part in / out → one row in PART IN-OUT (OUT = part used, write the customer).
- Payment (Home) or "Payment received" (Money page) → choose the customer / project from RECEIVABLES, amount, method (Cash, Bank, GCash, Check). Balance updates by itself. Partial payments are fine — just enter the amount received.
- New contract (Money page) → adds a new account to RECEIVABLES so payments can be tracked.
- Statement of account → Money → open the account → "Statement" → Print / PDF.
- Cleaning done → Customers → open the customer → "Cleaned" on the unit. Next cleaning = 6 months later, automatic.
- Book cleaning → Customers → open the customer → "Book cleaning" (or tap the cleaning item in Home "To do") → a cleaning job is filled in for the schedule.
- New installation (Customers page) → adds the installed unit to CUSTOMERS (customer, model, serial numbers, installed date). If it came from stock, also record Unit OUT.
- Quotation → Home "Quotation", or More → Quotes & Invoices → "New quotation" → add items from stock (price fills in from SRP / parts price), installation and other lines, discount, VAT → "Save & print / PDF". Number Q-YYYY-0001. "Status…" = Draft / Sent / Accepted / Lost.
- Invoice → Quotes & Invoices → "New invoice", or open a quotation → "Make invoice". Number INV-YYYY-0001, due date, payment details (printed on the invoice); it can add itself to Money (receivables). "Status…" = Unpaid / Partially paid / Paid / Cancelled.
- Giving a quotation / invoice / statement to the customer: tap "Save & print / PDF" (or "Statement") → in the print screen choose "Save as PDF" (on a phone: Share / Print → Save as PDF) → send the PDF by Viber, Messenger or email, or print it. The invoice layout is temporary until BRIZAIR's own invoice form is added.
- LG Monthly Report → More → LG Monthly Report → choose the month → daily IN / OUT grid like the old Excel → Print / PDF for LG.
- Ask GPT → this chat (English, Korean, Tagalog or Kapampangan; 👍 / 👎 on each answer). Materials for AI → upload price lists, brochures, rules, sample documents, photos; GPT reads them.
- Mistakes (rows are never deleted on the site):
  - Job, quotation, invoice → open it and fix it (job: "Edit"; quotation / invoice: change, then "Save & print / PDF").
  - Unit / part in-out → cannot be edited on the site. Record the opposite entry with a note, e.g. wrote OUT 2 but it was 1 → Unit in / out → IN 1, note "correction". Stock NOW becomes right.
  - Payment, new contract, new installation → cannot be edited on the site. Tell Jheena or Sir Kim; they fix the row in the Google Sheet (More → Open Google Sheet).
